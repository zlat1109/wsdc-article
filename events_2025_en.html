WSDC 2025: Events

Changes in the global top and regional trends.
The results of 2025 saw significant reshuffling in the top WSDC events. The key factor was the pause of MADjam — the largest event in recent years — which led to a redistribution of leading positions.

Competition Scale (Points)

The sum of all ranking points awarded at an event. This metric correlates with division size (Tier) and best reflects the competitive "weight" of the event.

Leadership in this category has shifted to Europe.

# Event Points Trend
1 ▲ +1 🇭🇺 BudaFest The year's leader by total points awarded. BudaFest 2025 awarded just 2 more points than BudaFest 2024, making the indirect competition with MADjam in 2026 even more interesting. 766 +0.3%
2 ▲ +1 🇺🇸 Wild Wild Westie Improved position, moving up from 3rd to 2nd place. 709 +3.8%
3 ▲ +3 🇺🇸 Boogie By The Bay Showed significant growth, moving up from 6th to 3rd place. 672 +11.8%
4 ▲ +7 🇺🇸 Liberty Swing Returned to the elite, moving up from 11th to 4th place. 633 +6.6%
5 🇺🇸 Atlanta Swing Classic Maintained stable position in the top. 632 +3.3%
6 ▲ +2 🇺🇸 Easter Swing One of the oldest US events moved up from 8th to 6th place. 622 +3.7%
7 🇺🇸 Swingtacular The California giant maintained its elite position. 620 +3.2%
8 ▲ +4 🇺🇸 J&J O'Rama The legendary event returned to the top ten, moving up from 12th to 8th place. 616 +5.8%

Note: Dropped out of the top 10: MADjam (paused in 2025), DC Swing eXperience (586 pts, 12th place) and French Open WCS (493 pts, ~30th place).

New Dancers (New)

The number of dancers who received their first points at an event. This metric reflects the inflow of new dancers.

In this category, traditional leader events give way to events with actively growing local communities.

# Event New Trend
1 🇸🇬 Asia WCS Open Remains the main entry point for new dancers in Asia, though 2024 saw more new dancers registered here (36 vs 32). 32 -8.6%
2 🇫🇷 West In Lyon France maintains strong positions in community growth dynamics. 27 +17.4%
3 🇷🇺 St.Petersburg WCS Nights Saw a massive surge in the number of dancers receiving their first points compared to 2024. 27 +125%
4 🇵🇱 King Swing Polish event showing active growth of the Polish community. 27 +12.5%
5 🇲🇾 MY Swing Malaysian event debuted in the global top, confirming WCS growth in the Asian region. 27 New

7 🇺🇸 SwingCouver Technically a Canadian brand, but effectively a major American event near Portland. After relocating, showed impressive growth in new dancers. 25 +127.3%

9 🇺🇸 Swing Over Orlando is emerging as a new major destination. 24 +41.2%

Note: There has been a major shake-up in this top 10. Compared to 2024, Swing City Chicago, Halloween SwingThing, Austin Rocks, German Open, DC Swing eXperience, Atlanta Swing Classic, Wild Wild Westie, and Trilogy Swing dropped out of the top ten. Only Asia WCS Open and Midnight Madness WCS remained in the top 10.
